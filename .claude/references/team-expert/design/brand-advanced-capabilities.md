# Brand Guardian - Advanced Capabilities

> **Domain**: Design / Brand Strategy
> **Last Updated**: 2026-03-19
> **Nguồn**: Brand strategy best practices từ Interbrand, Landor, Wolff Olins methodology

---

## Năng lực Nâng cao

### Competitive Positioning & Differentiation
- Phân tích competitive landscape để xác định white space
- Xây dựng brand differentiation strategy dựa trên unique value proposition
- Định vị thương hiệu trên perceptual map so với competitors

### Brand Architecture
- Thiết kế brand architecture cho complex product portfolios (Monolithic, Endorsed, Freestanding)
- Quản lý parent brand và sub-brand relationships
- Portfolio governance để tránh brand cannibalization

### International Brand Adaptation
- Localization strategy: adapt brand voice và visual elements theo từng market
- Cultural sensitivity review cho tên thương hiệu, màu sắc, hình ảnh
- Glocalization: giữ core identity, linh hoạt ở execution layer

### Brand Measurement
- Awareness metrics: unaided/aided recall, share of voice
- Sentiment tracking: NPS, CSAT, social listening signals
- Brand equity index: tổng hợp từ awareness, associations, perceived quality, loyalty
- KPI dashboard cho brand health monitoring

### Brand Refresh & Rebranding
- Trigger identification: khi nào cần refresh vs. rebrand toàn diện
- Quy trình brand refresh dựa trên market perception shifts và audience evolution
- Change management: rollout strategy để minimize confusion, maximize continuity

### Multi-Market Brand Governance
- Brand governance model: centralized vs. federated vs. hybrid
- Local adaptation framework: what can change, what must stay constant
- Brand compliance audit process cho distributed teams
