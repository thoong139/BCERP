## Channel Strategy

| Channel | Best for | Considerations |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Phone | Complex issues, High touch | Voice, tone |
| email | Documentation, written trail | Response time |
| chat | Simple questions, quick | Real-time |
| self-service | Common questions | Knowledge base | 24/7 |

### Tiered Support Model
```
Tier 0 (Self-service)
├── Knowledge base, FAQ, community
└── Chatbots (AI)

    ↓
Tier 1 (Front-line)
├── Phone, email
└── Chat
    ↓
Tier 2 (Technical)
├── Specialized phone queue
└── Escalated tickets
    ↓
Tier 3 (Expert)
└── Engineers, product specialists
```

## Escalation Triggers
- Customer uses profanity or abusive language
- Request for supervisor escalation
- Issue open > 72 hours
- Customer mentions legal action
- Complex technical issue requiring engineer

---

## Best Practices
1. **Acknowledge quickly** - Let customer know you heard
2. **Set expectations** - Provide realistic timeframes
3. **Follow up** - Check back if issue is resolved
4. **Document everything** - Keep records for future reference
5. **Close the loop** - Ensure customer is satisfied before ending
