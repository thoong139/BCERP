## 1. ISO 9001:2015 Clause Structure

ISO 9001:2015 gồm 10 clauses. Clauses 1-3 là giới thiệu; Clauses 4-10 là requirements bắt buộc.

### Clause 4: Context of the Organization

| Sub-clause | Requirement | Key Output |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| 4.1 | Internal & external issues affecting QMS | Issues register |
| 4.2 | Needs & expectations of interested parties | Stakeholder register |
| 4.3 | Scope of QMS | Documented QMS scope |
| 4.4 | QMS processes, interactions, criteria | Process map, turtle diagrams |

### Clause 5: Leadership

| Sub-clause | Requirement | Key Output |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| 5.1 | Top management commitment & accountability | Management review records |
| 5.2 | Quality Policy — appropriate, communicated, documented | Quality Policy document |
| 5.3 | Organizational roles, responsibilities, authorities | Org chart + RACI |

### Clause 6: Planning

| Sub-clause | Requirement | Key Output |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| 6.1 | Risks & opportunities (risk-based thinking) | Risk register |
| 6.2 | Quality objectives — SMART, measured, communicated | Quality Objectives table |
| 6.3 | Planning of changes — systematic, considered | Change request log |

### Clause 7: Support

| Sub-clause | Requirement | Key Output |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| 7.1 | Resources (people, infrastructure, environment, monitoring) | Resource plan |
| 7.2 | Competence — determine, ensure, maintain evidence | Training matrix, records |
| 7.3 | Awareness — policy, objectives, contribution, NCR | Communication records |
| 7.4 | Communication — what, when, with whom, how | Communication plan |
| 7.5 | Documented Information — create, control, retain | Document master list |

### Clause 8: Operation

| Sub-clause | Requirement | Key Output |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| 8.1 | Operational planning & control | Process control plans |
| 8.2 | Requirements for products & services (customer, statutory) | Requirements review |
| 8.3 | Design & development (if applicable) | Design records, DFMEA |
| 8.4 | Control of external providers (suppliers) | Supplier approval, AVL |
| 8.5 | Production & service provision | Work instructions, traceability |
| 8.6 | Release of products & services | Inspection records, sign-off |
| 8.7 | Control of nonconforming outputs | NCR, disposition records |

### Clause 9: Performance Evaluation

| Sub-clause | Requirement | Key Output |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| 9.1 | Monitoring, measurement, analysis | KPI dashboard, data analysis |
| 9.2 | Internal audit | Audit program, audit reports |
| 9.3 | Management review | Management review minutes, actions |

### Clause 10: Improvement

| Sub-clause | Requirement | Key Output |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| 10.1 | General improvement (PDCA) | Improvement projects |
| 10.2 | NCR & CAPA — react, evaluate, root cause, prevent recurrence | NCR/CAPA register |
| 10.3 | Continual improvement — suitability, adequacy, effectiveness | Improvement opportunities log |

## 2. QMS Documentation Requirements

### Mandatory Documented Information (phải có)

**Documented Procedures (phải duy trì):**
- Quality Policy (Clause 5.2)
- Quality Objectives (Clause 6.2)
- QMS Scope (Clause 4.3)
- Evidence of competence (Clause 7.2)
- Any other documented information required by the standard

**Mandatory Records (phải retain):**

| Record | Clause | Minimum Retention |
|--------|--------|-------------------|
| Monitoring & measurement results | 9.1 | Per quality objective period |
| Internal audit results | 9.2 | 3 years minimum |
| Management review results | 9.3 | 3 years minimum |
| Evidence of competence | 7.2 | Employment + 3 years |
| Product/service requirements review | 8.2 | Per product lifecycle |
| Design & development outputs | 8.3 | Per product lifecycle |
| Nonconforming outputs | 8.7 | 3 years minimum |
| CAPA evidence | 10.2 | 5 years minimum |
| Calibration records | 7.1.5 | Equipment lifetime |

### Document Control Requirements (Clause 7.5)

- **Identification**: document number, revision, date, author
- **Format & media**: appropriate for use (digital, paper, or both)
- **Review & approval**: before issue; re-approval after change
- **Version control**: current version clearly identified; obsolete version controlled
- **Distribution**: available where needed, protected from unintended alteration
- **Retention**: defined retention period per record type

## 3. Quality Objectives (Clause 6.2)

### SMART Criteria

| Criterion | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Specific** | Rõ ràng, không mơ hồ | "Giảm NCR closure time" (không phải "cải thiện quality") |
| **Measurable** | Có metric cụ thể | "Giảm 20% NCR cycle time" |
| **Achievable** | Khả thi với nguồn lực | Dựa trên benchmark + capability |
| **Relevant** | Liên kết với business objectives | "Tăng customer satisfaction" |
| **Time-bound** | Có deadline | "Đến Q4 2026" |

### Quality Objectives Examples by Sector

| Sector | Objective | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Manufacturing | First Pass Yield (FPY) | ≥ 97% |
| Software | Escaped defects rate | < 0.5 per release |
| Healthcare | Adverse event rate | < 2 per 1,000 procedures |
| Logistics | On-time delivery | ≥ 98.5% |
| General | CAPA closure on time | ≥ 90% within due date |
| General | Customer complaint rate | < 0.3% of transactions |

## 4. Management Review (Clause 9.3)

### Minimum Frequency: Annual (thực tế best practice: Quarterly)

### Required Inputs

| Input | Source |
|-------|--------|
| Customer satisfaction & feedback | Survey, complaint data, NPS |
| Quality objectives performance | KPI dashboard |
| Process performance, product conformity | Process data, inspection results |
| NCR & CAPA status | NCR/CAPA register |
| Internal and external audit results | Audit program |
| Supplier performance | Supplier quality KPIs |
| Risks & opportunities status | Risk register |
| Resources adequacy | Resource plan |
| Recommendations for improvement | Team input |
| Actions from previous reviews | Action tracker |

### Required Outputs (phải documented)

- Decisions on improvement opportunities
- Any need for changes to QMS
- Resource needs

## 5. Internal Audit (Clause 9.2)

### Audit Program Planning (Risk-based)

Frequency và scope phụ thuộc vào:
- Importance of process to QMS and product quality
- Previous audit results (repeat findings = more frequent)
- Changes in process, technology, personnel
- Customer or regulatory requirements

### Audit Process Steps

```
1. Plan: Define scope, criteria, auditor (competent, objective)
2. Notify: Advance notice, request documents
3. Document Review: Review procedures, records (off-site)
4. Opening Meeting: Introduce scope, method, confidentiality
5. Field Work: Observe, interview, collect evidence
6. Finding Analysis: Classify findings (Observation / Minor NC / Major NC)
7. Closing Meeting: Present findings, get auditee acknowledgement
8. Audit Report: Written within 5 business days
9. CAR Issue: For NC findings — auditee must respond
10. Follow-up: Verify corrective actions, close CAR
```

### Finding Classification

| Classification | Definition | CAR Required | Response Time |
|---------------|------------|:------------:|:-------------:|
| **Observation** | Minor concern, potential for improvement | No | Voluntary |
| **Minor NC** | Isolated failure, no systemic evidence | Yes | 30 days |
| **Major NC** | Systemic failure, total absence of required element | Yes | 7-14 days |

**Major NC examples:**
- No documented quality policy
- No internal audit program
- No CAPA system
- Repeated minor NC from previous audit without correction
