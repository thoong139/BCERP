## 2. Chi Tiết Từng Kênh

### 2.1 Google Ads

**Best use cases:**
- Capture existing demand (người đang tìm kiếm sản phẩm/dịch vụ)
- Local business, lead generation, e-commerce

**Targeting capabilities:**
- Keywords (exact, phrase, broad match)
- Audiences: In-market, Affinity, Custom Intent, Remarketing
- Demographics, Device, Location, Ad schedule

**Ad formats:**
- Search: Text ads, Responsive Search Ads (RSA)
- Display: Banner, Responsive Display Ads
- Shopping: Product Listing Ads, Performance Max
- Video: YouTube In-stream, Bumper, Discovery
- Performance Max: tự động cross-channel

**Budget guidance:** Tối thiểu $30–50/ngày cho Search để đủ data optimization.

---

### 2.2 Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

**Best use cases:**
- Brand awareness, product discovery
- Retargeting website visitors
- B2C với visual product (fashion, beauty, food, travel)

**Targeting capabilities:**
- Custom Audiences: Customer list, Website visitors, App users
- Lookalike Audiences (1–10% similarity)
- Interest, Behavior, Demographics
- Advantage+ Audience (AI-driven)

**Ad formats:**
- Image, Video, Carousel, Collection
- Reels Ads, Stories Ads
- Instant Experience (fullscreen mobile)
- Lead Ads (in-app form)

**Budget guidance:** Tối thiểu $20/ngày per ad set; cần ít nhất 50 conversions/tuần để exit learning phase.

---

### 2.3 LinkedIn Ads

**Best use cases:**
- B2B lead generation
- Enterprise software, SaaS, recruitment, training
- Account-based marketing (ABM)

**Targeting capabilities:**
- Job title, Job function, Seniority
- Company name, Company size, Industry
- Skills, Groups membership
- Matched Audiences (list upload, retargeting)

**Ad formats:**
- Sponsored Content (single image, video, carousel, document)
- Message Ads / Conversation Ads (InMail)
- Lead Gen Forms (in-platform)
- Dynamic Ads (personalized)
- Text Ads (right rail)

**Budget guidance:** Tối thiểu $50–100/ngày; CPL thường $50–200 cho B2B leads chất lượng.

---

### 2.4 TikTok Ads

**Best use cases:**
- Brand awareness với Gen Z, Millennial
- Product discovery và viral potential
- FMCG, fashion, beauty, gaming, entertainment

**Targeting capabilities:**
- Demographics, Interests, Behaviors
- Custom Audiences, Lookalike
- Hashtag Targeting (Topview)
- Creator Audience (Spark Ads)

**Ad formats:**
- In-Feed Ads (native video)
- TopView (first video khi mở app)
- Branded Hashtag Challenge
- Spark Ads (boost organic content)
- Collection Ads

**Budget guidance:** Tối thiểu $50/ngày ở campaign level; creative chạy được 3–5 ngày trước khi test mới.

---

### 2.5 Microsoft Ads

**Best use cases:**
- Bổ sung traffic từ Bing/Yahoo (thường ít cạnh tranh hơn Google)
- B2B, Finance, Healthcare, older demographics (45+)

**Targeting capabilities:** Tương tự Google Ads + LinkedIn Profile targeting
**Ad formats:** Search, Shopping, Audience Network (display)
**Budget guidance:** CPC thường thấp hơn Google 20–40%; dùng import từ Google Ads để tiết kiệm thời gian setup.

---

### 2.6 Amazon Ads

**Best use cases:**
- Brand selling trực tiếp trên Amazon marketplace
- CPG, electronics, books, toys

**Ad formats:**
- Sponsored Products (search results)
- Sponsored Brands (header banner)
- Sponsored Display (retargeting on/off Amazon)
- DSP (programmatic display)

**Budget guidance:** ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales) target < Gross Margin %; bắt đầu với Sponsored Products manual bidding.

### Decision Guide theo Business Type

| Business Type | Primary Channel | Secondary | Avoid |
|---------------|----------------|-----------|-------|
| B2B SaaS | LinkedIn | Google Search | TikTok, Amazon |
| B2C E-commerce | Meta Ads | Google Shopping | LinkedIn |
| Local Service | Google Search | Meta Ads (geo) | LinkedIn, TikTok |
| DTC Fashion | Meta/Instagram | TikTok | Microsoft, Amazon |
| Enterprise Software | LinkedIn | Google Search | TikTok |
| CPG / FMCG | Meta Ads | TikTok, Amazon | LinkedIn |
| Recruitment | LinkedIn | Meta Ads | Amazon |

## 4. Budget Allocation Framework

### Quy Tắc 70/20/10

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                BUDGET SPLIT                │
├────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  70% — Core Channels (proven performance)  │
│         Kênh đang có ROAS/CPA tốt          │
│                                            │
│  20% — Growth Channels (scaling tests)     │
│         Kênh đang test và có tín hiệu tốt  │
│                                            │
│  10% — Experimental (new platforms)        │
│         Kênh hoàn toàn mới, chưa có data   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Budget Allocation theo Funnel Stage

| Funnel Stage | Mục Tiêu | % Budget | Kênh Phù Hợp |
|--------------|----------|----------|--------------|
| Awareness (TOFU) | Reach, Impressions | 20–30% | Display, YouTube, TikTok |
| Consideration (MOFU) | Clicks, Engagement | 30–40% | Search, Meta, LinkedIn |
| Conversion (BOFU) | Leads, Sales | 40–50% | Search Remarketing, Shopping |
| Retention | Repeat Purchase, LTV | 10–15% | Meta Custom Audience, Email |

## 5. Seasonal Budgeting Patterns

| Mùa / Sự Kiện | Thời Điểm | Budget Adjustment | Ghi Chú |
|---------------|-----------|------------------|---------|
| Q4 Holiday (Black Friday, Giáng Sinh) | Tháng 10–12 | +50–150% | CPM tăng mạnh; chuẩn bị creative sớm |
| Tết Nguyên Đán (VN) | Tháng 1–2 | +40–80% | Mobile-first, video content |
| Back to School | Tháng 8–9 | +20–40% | E-learning, stationery, electronics |
| Valentine's Day | Đầu tháng 2 | +20–30% | Gift, fashion, F&B |
| Mid-Year Sale (6/6, 7/7) | Tháng 6–7 | +30–60% | E-commerce focus |
| Low Season (Jan, Post-holiday) | Tháng 1, 3 | -20–30% | Test và optimize; lower CPM là cơ hội |

**Nguyên tắc:** Tăng budget trước peak season 2–3 tuần để thuật toán học đủ trước khi cần deliver volume.
