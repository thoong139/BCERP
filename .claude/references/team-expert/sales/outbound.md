## 1. Signal-Based Selling Framework

Outreach triggered bởi buying signals convert 4-8x so với untriggered cold outreach.

### Signal Categories (xếp theo Intent Strength)

| Tier | Loại Signal | Ví dụ |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Tier 1 — Active Buying** (ưu tiên cao nhất) | Direct intent | G2/review site visits, pricing page views, competitor comparison searches, RFP announcements |
| **Tier 2 — Organizational Change** | Leadership/structure changes | VP mới = priorities mới; Funding Series B+ = budget + urgency; Hiring surge = scaling pain; M&A = tool consolidation |
| **Tier 3 — Technographic/Behavioral** | Tech stack & content engagement | Technology stack changes (BuiltWith, job postings), conference attendance, whitepaper downloads, competitor renewal timing |

### Speed-to-Signal

| Thời gian | Trạng thái signal |
|-----------|-------------------|
| <30 phút | Fresh — route đến rep ngay |
| <24 giờ | Warm — vẫn actionable |
| >72 giờ | Stale — competitor đã có conversation |

> Build routing rules match signal type → rep expertise + territory. Không để signals nằm trong shared queue.

## 2. ICP Definition (Ideal Customer Profile)

ICP hữu ích phải **falsifiable**. Nếu không exclude companies nào → không phải ICP, chỉ là TAM slide.

### ICP Framework

```
FIRMOGRAPHIC FILTERS
- Industry verticals: [2-4 cụ thể, không phải "enterprise"]
- Revenue range hoặc employee count band
- Geography (nếu relevant)
- Technology stack requirements (đã dùng tool gì)

BEHAVIORAL QUALIFIERS
- Business event nào khiến họ là buyer RIGHT NOW?
- Pain nào product solve mà họ không thể ignore?
- Ai trong org cảm nhận pain rõ nhất?
- Workaround hiện tại trông như thế nào?

DISQUALIFIERS (quan trọng ngang)
- Điều gì khiến account look good on paper nhưng thực tế không close?
- Industries/segments có win rate <15%
- Company stages mà product premature hoặc overkill
```

## 3. Tiered Account Engagement Model

| Tier | Số lượng | Personalization | Contacts/Account | Approach |
|------|----------|-----------------|-------------------|----------|
| **Tier 1** (Top) | 50-100 | Deep, multi-threaded | 3-5 | Full account research (10-K, earnings calls), custom messaging per persona, direct mail, warm intros |
| **Tier 2** (Mid) | 200-500 | Semi-personalized | 2-3 | Industry-specific messaging + account-level opening line, signal-triggered enrollment |
| **Tier 3** (Scale) | ICP-fit còn lại | Automated + light personalization | 1 | Industry/role-based sequences, dynamic tokens, signal-triggered only |

### Tier Promotion/Demotion

- Quarterly review: promote Tier 3 → Tier 2 nếu engagement cao
- Demote Tier 2 → Tier 3 nếu không response sau 2 sequences
- Tier 1 accounts có weekly strategy reviews

## 4. Multi-Channel Sequence Architecture

### Channel Selection by Persona

| Persona | Primary | Secondary | Tertiary |
|---------|---------|-----------|----------|
| C-Suite | LinkedIn (InMail) | Warm intro / referral | Short, direct email |
| VP-level | Email | LinkedIn | Phone |
| Director | Email | Phone | LinkedIn |
| Manager / IC | Email | LinkedIn | Video (Loom) |
| Technical buyers | Email (technical content) | Community/Slack | LinkedIn |

### Sequence Template (8-12 touches, 3-4 tuần)

| Touch | Day | Channel | Nội dung |
|-------|-----|---------|----------|
| 1 | 1 | Email | Signal-based opening + specific value prop + soft CTA |
| 2 | 3 | LinkedIn | Connection request với personalized note (no pitch) |
| 3 | 5 | Email | Share relevant insight/data point tied to situation |
| 4 | 8 | Phone | Call + voicemail drop referencing email thread |
| 5 | 10 | LinkedIn | Engage with their content hoặc share relevant content |
| 6 | 14 | Email | Case study từ similar company/situation + clear CTA |
| 7 | 17 | Video | 60-second personalized Loom showing something specific |
| 8 | 21 | Email | New angle — different pain point hoặc stakeholder perspective |
| 9 | 24 | Phone | Final call attempt |
| 10 | 28 | Email | Breakup email — honest, brief, leave door open |

> Mỗi touch cần thêm value angle mới. Repeat cùng ask bằng words khác = nagging, không phải sequence.

## 5. Cold Email Anatomy

### Structure

```
SUBJECT LINE
- 3-5 words, lowercase, looks like internal email
- Reference signal hoặc specificity: "re: the new data team"
- Never clickbait, never ALL CAPS, never emoji

OPENING LINE (Personalized, Signal-Based)
Bad:  "I hope this email finds you well."
Bad:  "I'm reaching out because [company] helps companies like yours..."
Good: "Saw you just hired 4 data engineers — scaling analytics team
       usually means current tooling hitting ceiling."

VALUE PROPOSITION (In Buyer's Language)
- 1 câu connecting their situation → outcome they care about
- Dùng vocabulary của họ, không phải marketing copy
- Specificity > cleverness: numbers, timeframes, outcomes

SOCIAL PROOF (Optional, 1 Line)
- "[Similar company] cut [metric] by [number] in [timeframe]"
- Chỉ include nếu genuinely relevant

CTA (Single, Clear, Low Friction)
Bad:  "Would love to set up 30-minute call to walk through demo"
Good: "Worth 15-minute conversation to see if this applies?"
Good: "Open to hearing how [similar company] handled this?"
```

### Reply Rate Benchmarks

| Quality Tier | Reply Rate |
|-------------|------------|
| Generic, untargeted | 1-3% |
| Role/industry personalized | 5-8% |
| Signal-based + account research | 12-25% |
| Warm introduction / referral | 30-50% |

## 7. Rules of Engagement

- Không gửi outreach mà không có reason buyer should care RIGHT NOW
- Nếu không articulate được tại sao contact this specific person, at this specific company, at this specific moment → chưa sẵn sàng gửi
- Respect opt-outs immediately và completely — non-negotiable
- Không automate cái nên personal, không personalize cái nên automated
- Test 1 variable at a time — change cả subject + opening + CTA = learn nothing
- Document what works — playbook trong đầu 1 rep không phải playbook
