## 1. Net Revenue Retention (NRR) — Metric tối thượng

NRR captures expansion, contraction, và churn trong 1 con số duy nhất.

```
NRR = (Beginning ARR + Expansion - Contraction - Churn) / Beginning ARR × 100%
```

| NRR Range | Đánh giá |
|-----------|----------|
| >130% | Excellent — growth engine từ existing customers |
| 120-130% | Strong — healthy expansion |
| 100-120% | Adequate — expansion offset churn |
| <100% | Leaky bucket — churn > expansion |

> Optimize cho NRR, không phải bookings. NRR compound over time.

## 2. Land-and-Expand Execution

### Expansion Signal Types

| Signal | Ý nghĩa | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Capacity threshold (>80% license consumption) | Usage hitting ceiling | Upsell conversation |
| Feature adoption velocity cao | Deep engagement | Cross-sell adjacent modules |
| Department-level usage asymmetry | Adoption uneven | Expand to underserved departments |
| Headcount growth trong buyer's team | Scaling pain | License expansion |
| New strategic initiative announced | New budget/priority | Solution alignment conversation |

### Expansion Signal Discipline

Mỗi signal cần paired với:
1. **Context**: Tại sao điều này xảy ra?
2. **Timing**: Tại sao bây giờ?
3. **Stakeholder alignment**: Ai care?

> Thiếu 1 trong 3 → observation, không phải opportunity.

### Rule: Không sell vào unhealthy account

> Selling more vào account chưa successful với cái đang có = accelerate churn, không phải growth.

### Expansion Readiness vs Expansion Intent

| Readiness (could buy more) | Intent (wants to buy more) |
|----------------------------|----------------------------|
| Usage high, capacity available | Buyer asked about new modules |
| Budget cycle coming up | Champion building internal business case |
| New department head | Evaluation criteria being drafted |

> Chỉ Intent convert reliably. Readiness cần nurturing.

## 3. Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR)

### Nguyên tắc: Forward-looking strategic planning, KHÔNG backward-looking status reports

### QBR Structure (60 phút)

| Phase | Thời gian | Nội dung |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Value Delivered** | 15 phút | ROI recap với hard numbers — time saved, revenue generated, cost avoided |
| **Their Roadmap** | 20 phút | "Business đi đâu trong 12 tháng tới? Chúng tôi nên evolve thế nào?" |
| **Product Alignment** | 15 phút | How we evolve together — tied to their priorities |
| **Mutual Action Plan** | 10 phút | Commitments từ cả 2 phía, owners, dates |

### QBR Questions to Ask

- "Top 3 business priorities cho 2 quarters tới?"
- "Đâu đang tốn thời gian vào manual work nên được automated?"
- "Ai khác trong org đang cố solve similar problems?"
- "Cần gì để bạn confident mở rộng partnership?"

### QBR Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Usage Trends: key metrics, adoption curves, capacity utilization
- [ ] Support History: ticket volume, CSAT, escalations
- [ ] ROI Data: quantified value — specific numbers, not estimates
- [ ] Industry Context: market conditions, competitive pressures
- [ ] Attendee confirmation: roles, new faces to map
- [ ] Missing stakeholders: ai nên có mặt nhưng không attend, tại sao

## 4. Stakeholder Mapping & Multi-Threading

### Stakeholder Map Template

```
| Name | Title | Role | Influence | Sentiment | Last Contact |
|------|-------|------|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| [Name] | [Title] | Champion | High | Positive | [Date] |
| [Name] | [Title] | Economic Buyer | High | Neutral | [Date] |
| [Name] | [Title] | End User | Medium | Positive | [Date] |
| [Name] | [Title] | Detractor | Medium | Negative | [Date] |
```

### Stakeholder Roles

| Role | Mô tả | Tại sao quan trọng |
|------|--------|---------------------|
| **Champion** | Internal advocate có power + access + personal motivation | Sell internally khi bạn không trong phòng |
| **Economic Buyer** | Control budget, nói "có" khi tất cả nói "không" | Final decision authority |
| **Influencer** | Technical evaluator, end user có opinion | Có thể block hoặc accelerate |
| **Detractor** | Người phản đối — có thể ẩn | Kill expansion ở last mile nếu không biết |
| **Coach** | Friendly nhưng ít power — share info | Valuable intel nhưng không drive decisions |

### Multi-Threading Rules

- Ít nhất **3 independent relationship threads** per account
- Nếu champion rời đi ngày mai → vẫn phải có active conversations
- Map **informal influence network**, không chỉ org chart
- Track detractors cẩn thận như track champions

## 5. Account Health Scoring

### Health Score Components

| Component | Signals | Weight |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| **Product Usage** | Active users, feature adoption, capacity utilization | 30% |
| **Support Sentiment** | Ticket volume trend, CSAT, escalations | 20% |
| **Stakeholder Engagement** | Meeting frequency, multi-threading depth | 20% |
| **Contract Timeline** | Days to renewal, expansion conversations | 15% |
| **Executive Sponsor Activity** | Last contact, engagement level | 15% |

### Health-Based Playbooks

| Health | Playbook | Focus |
|--------|----------|-------|
| 🟢 Green | Expansion play | Identify whitespace, upsell/cross-sell |
| 🟡 Yellow | Stabilization play | Rebuild engagement, demonstrate value |
| 🔴 Red | Save play | Intervention, executive involvement |

> **KHÔNG BAO GIỜ** chạy expansion play trên red account.

## 6. Churn Prevention

### Early Warning Signals

| Signal | Threshold | Severity |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Monthly active users declining | <baseline | Medium → High |
| Feature adoption (core) | <50% | High |
| Executive sponsor engagement | >60 ngày no contact | High |
| Support ticket sentiment | <3.5/5 | Medium |
| Champion status | Departed / At risk | Critical |

### Intervention Plan Template

```
# Churn Prevention: [Account Name]

## Risk Assessment
- Probability of churn: [%] với rationale
- Revenue at risk: [$]
- Save difficulty: [Low/Medium/High]
- Investment to save: [Hours, resources, exec involvement]

## Intervention
- Immediate (this week): [Stabilize]
- Short-term (30 ngày): [Rebuild engagement, demonstrate value]
- Medium-term (90 ngày): [Re-establish strategic alignment]
```

### Churn Precursors (Leading Indicators)

- Declining usage + executive sponsor departure → highest churn predictor
- Loss of champion + no multi-threading → single point of failure
- Support escalation pattern + declining CSAT → erosion of trust
- Competitor engagement signals + contract renewal approaching → active evaluation

## 8. Relationship Integrity Principles

- Không sacrifice relationship cho transaction. Deal push quá mạnh hôm nay = mất 3 deals trong 2 năm tới.
- Honest về product limitations. Customers trust candor → cho access và budget nhiều hơn.
- Expansion nên feel like natural next step, không phải sales motion. Nếu customer surprised bởi ask → chưa làm groundwork.
- Mọi expansion opportunity cần có documented business case **từ perspective của customer**, không phải của mình.
